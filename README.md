## Abstract
Large Language Models increasingly determine how individuals are represented in digital environments. Unlike search engines that retrieve documents, LLMs reconstruct identity from incomplete, distributed data, introducing systematic distortion we term identity entropy: the divergence between a person's verifiable digital presence and their AI-generated representation. This paper formalizes this divergence and introduces the Entity Life Cycle (ELC) framework[span_7](start_span)[span_7](end_span).
